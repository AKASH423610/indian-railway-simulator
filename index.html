DRIVER HANDBOOK

Operating the locomotive

1. Choose WAP-7, WAP-5, WAG-9, WAG-12, WDP-4, WDM-2 or WDM-3A in SELECT LOCO.

2. On electric locomotives, raise the pantograph with P or the button, then start traction. On diesel locomotives, start the engine directly. Increase traction using the slider or W / Up Arrow.

3. Apply the service brake using the slider or S / Down Arrow. X applies the emergency brake. Use R to reverse only after stopping.

4. Passenger engines couple passenger coaches; WAG freight engines couple freight wagons.

5. Use V to cycle Chase, Front, Rear, Coupling close-up and Driver cab views. Press O to open or close the cab side doors.

6. Select MAP SELECT or press M to travel to Chennai Beach, Avadi, Arakkonam countryside or Jolarpettai hill section. Stop before changing route.

This is an evolving browser-based 3D simulator. Vehicle systems and physics are still being expanded.
